03

What it carries

The asset is what it remembers.

A rented model forgets. The brain keeps what the bank learns: the decisions, the precedents, the house style, and the reason behind every credit call. That memory, governed and scoped, is the part the bank owns.

  • 01
    Decision ledger

    Every hop is designed to be policy-checked and recorded. The why behind every credit decision is kept, not just the outcome, so a call can be explained and challenged later.

  • 02
    Institutional memory

    An approved memo is a vote. A correction is a lesson. Signals accumulate into the bank's own house style rather than resetting with every session.

  • 03
    Tiered memory

    Scoped by altitude: this deal, then the portfolio, then bank-wide policy. Each retrieval draws the right context at the right level, never the whole book at once.

  • 04
    Governed retrieval

    Precedent found by meaning, not by filename, then passed through a curation gate before it is reused. Retrieval is a governed step, not a free-for-all search.
