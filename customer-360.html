The commercial credit lifecycle

Commercial credit runs a six-stage lifecycle, from first contact to servicing the booked loan. Customer 360 is the relationship lens that stays open across every stage, so the banker meets each conversation with the whole obligor group already resolved. KYC, collateral and covenant run as through-lines the whole way.

The six-stage lifecycle Customer 360 is live at every stage. nCino is the system of record from Sales onward.

07
Under the hood

Read in place. nCino stays the system of record.

The cockpit reads the relationship live from where the bank already keeps it, through typed MCP connectors. A graph resolves one borrower, one guarantor and one exposure across those systems into a single identity, which is exactly what the constellation draws.

Read, resolved, rendered. The obligor group and relationship come from Salesforce, the deal, facilities, covenants and collateral from nCino, servicing from AFS and spreads from the spreading engine, all retrieved through typed MCP connectors. Everything is read in place, under the bank's existing permissions, with no duplicate system of record.
Where this lives tomorrow

Seeing the relationship whole is one capability. When the same resolved view feeds the Commercial Credit Brain, which remembers this borrower deal to deal and carries the lesson from one relationship into the next, the cockpit stops being a screen and becomes memory.
