06

Under the hood

Drafted outside, decided inside nCino.

A drafting agent reads structured deal data from nCino, pulls spreads from the spreading engine and servicing from AFS, and assembles the memo. A separate reviewer performs effective challenge, kept apart from drafting.

DRAFT decision-support. The committee decides. Guided underwriting skills mirror the nCino workflow steps: loan setup, terms and covenants, risk rating, policy exceptions, pricing, credit binder, decision notice. Write-back to nCino: narrative sync to the memo fields, nFORMS publish, submit for approval. No bulk migration and no duplicate system of record. Data is read in place under the bank's existing permissions. nCino stays the system of record.

Where this lives tomorrow

One memo is one capability. The same drafting skill, the same sourcing discipline and the same governed write-back become part of a larger asset: a brain that carries what it learns on this deal into the next.
